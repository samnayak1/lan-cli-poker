# People photo credits

The faces of the guests and staff in the browser GUI's casino are cropped from the photos
below (bodies are drawn). Every source photo is released under **CC0 / public domain** on
Wikimedia Commons (originally published on Unsplash or Pexels). Backgrounds were removed with
[rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) (`u2net_human_seg`) and faces located with OpenCV.
